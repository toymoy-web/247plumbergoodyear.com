Peoria is one of the larger cities in the West Valley, and its housing stock ranges from established southern neighborhoods to rapidly developing areas further north, often within the same day's service route. Arizona's climate adds its own layer on top of that: extreme summer heat and the annual monsoon season both show up in the plumbing calls we get here in predictable ways.

By Season

How Arizona's Seasons Show Up In Peoria Plumbing Calls.

Peak Summer Heat.

Extreme summer temperatures push water heaters harder and can accelerate wear on already-aging units, especially tank units already past the ten-year mark.

Monsoon Season.

Summer monsoon storms occasionally cause sewer backups in low-lying areas after heavy, fast rainfall overwhelms older drainage capacity.

Winter Usage Patterns.

Cooler months bring less strain on water heaters but more indoor water use overall, which can expose an existing slow leak that summer usage patterns had been masking.

Year-Round Hard Water.

Unlike seasonal issues, mineral content in the water supply is a constant, not a seasonal factor, and it compounds whatever else a season brings.

If You Have A Burst Pipe Or Active Leak.

Shut off the main water valve if you can safely reach it, then call, our emergency line runs 24/7.
