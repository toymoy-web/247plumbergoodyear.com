Most people call about a leak they can't actually see. The water bill jumped, the meter keeps spinning with everything in the house shut off, or there's a warm or damp spot on the floor with no obvious source. Leak detection is about finding exactly where the water is going before anyone starts cutting into drywall, flooring, or concrete.

Benefits

Why Electronic Detection Instead Of Guesswork.

Pinpoint Accuracy.

Acoustic and thermal equipment locates the leak within a small area instead of an entire wall or slab.

Minimal Damage.

Finding the exact spot means only the affected section needs to be opened up.

Works Through Concrete.

Slab leaks are detected without guessing where to jackhammer first.

Clear Next Step.

You get a specific repair plan, not just confirmation that a leak exists.

Common Signs

Signs You Have A Hidden Leak.

Water Bill Jumped Unexpectedly.

A sudden increase with no change in household water use.

Meter Still Moving, Water Off.

If every fixture is off and the meter still spins, water is escaping somewhere in the line.

Warm Spot On The Floor.

Often a sign of a hot water line leaking under a slab.

Damp Spot Or Mildew Smell.

A wall or ceiling that stays damp without an obvious plumbing fixture nearby.

Slab leaks show up often enough in Goodyear's slab-on-grade construction that a warm patch of flooring or a spike in the water bill is one of the more common reasons people call us before they've even confirmed a leak exists. Copper supply lines under the slab in 1990s-era neighborhoods like PebbleCreek are the most frequent source once a home reaches that age, and it's a pattern we see just as often in Sun City's even older original construction.

FAQs

Common Questions.

Do you have the actual equipment to find a leak I can't see?

Yes, we use acoustic and thermal leak detection equipment rather than guessing based on visible symptoms alone.

Can you find a leak under a concrete slab?

Yes, slab leak detection is one of our most common calls in Goodyear's slab-on-grade homes, including in older Litchfield Park construction.

Will you need to cut into my walls or floor just to look?

No, detection equipment is used first specifically to avoid unnecessary demolition.

What if the meter keeps running even with the water off?

That's one of the clearest signs of a leak in the line and worth calling about even before you've seen any water.

Do you handle commercial properties too?

Yes, we detect leaks for commercial properties, including at the meter and along interior lines.

A leak that hasn't shown itself yet is still worth acting on, since the cost of detection is almost always smaller than the cost of the damage a slab or wall leak causes the longer it runs undetected. We treat detection as its own separate step from repair, which means you get a clear answer on where the water is going before committing to any repair cost, and you're never paying to fix something based on a guess. If the leak turns out to be tied to the water heater rather than the slab itself, that's a separate, usually simpler fix.
