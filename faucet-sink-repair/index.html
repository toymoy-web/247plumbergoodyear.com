Common Problems

Signs You Need Faucet Or Sink Repair.

Constant Dripping.

Almost always a worn cartridge or washer inside the faucet body.

Leak Under The Sink.

Usually a supply line, shutoff valve, or P-trap connection worth checking before it damages the cabinet.

Low Or Uneven Water Pressure.

Can be a clogged aerator, though sometimes points to a larger supply issue.

Loose Or Wobbly Faucet.

A worn mounting base, usually a quick fix before it worsens.

Hard water across the West Valley shortens the life of faucet cartridges faster than in soft-water regions, so a faucet that's dripped for months usually has more mineral buildup inside than a quick wipe-down will fix. This shows up on both kitchen and bathroom fixtures roughly the same way, regardless of which room the faucet is in or how often it gets used, and it's the same reason a softener often comes up in the same conversation.

FAQs

Common Questions.

Can you fix a dripping faucet without replacing the whole thing?

Usually yes, most drips are a cartridge or washer repair rather than a full replacement, similar to a quick disposal fix while we're already under the sink.

Do you install faucets I've purchased myself?

Yes, we can install a customer-supplied faucet as well as ones we provide.

Is a leak under my kitchen sink urgent?

It's worth a prompt call, since ongoing moisture can damage the cabinet base over time.

Can you match a specific faucet finish or brand during installation?

Yes, we install customer-chosen fixtures and can also recommend options if you're not set on a specific brand.

Do you repair pull-down or pull-out kitchen faucet sprayers?

Yes, sprayer hose and diverter issues are part of standard faucet repair, not a separate service.

Do you repair both kitchen and bathroom faucets?

Yes, the same service covers both.

The math on a dripping faucet is easy to underestimate: a slow, steady drip adds up to real wasted water and a higher bill over a year, for a repair that usually takes less than an hour once a technician is on site. Under-sink leaks are worth acting on faster, since a cabinet base that's been wet for weeks is a different, more expensive repair than the valve that caused it. West Valley hard water shortens cartridge life noticeably compared to soft-water regions. That's part of why the same faucet model can drip again sooner here than the manufacturer's typical lifespan would suggest, and why we check for mineral buildup as a matter of course rather than only replacing the obvious worn part. The same visit is a good time to bundle in a running toilet if you've been meaning to deal with one anyway.
