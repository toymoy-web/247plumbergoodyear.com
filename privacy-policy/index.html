Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Goodyear 24/7 Plumbing ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you contact us by phone, email, or through this website.

Information We Collect.

When you call or email us, we collect the information you provide, such as your name, phone number, email address, service address, and details about the plumbing issue you're contacting us about. We do not use tracking forms or lead-capture widgets on this website. All initial contact happens by phone or email.

How We Use Your Information.

We use the information you provide to schedule and perform plumbing services, communicate with you about appointments and repairs, and maintain records for warranty and billing purposes. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Information Sharing.

We may share your information with subcontractors or suppliers directly involved in completing a service you've requested, or as required by law. We do not share your information with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

Website Analytics.

This website may use standard web analytics tools to understand general traffic patterns, such as which pages are visited. This data is aggregated and is not used to identify individual visitors.

Data Security.

We take reasonable steps to protect the information you share with us. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your Choices.

You can contact us at any time to ask what information we have on file for you, or to request that we delete records not required to be retained for legal or warranty purposes.

Changes To This Policy.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

Contact Us.

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at contact@247plumbergoodyear.com or call (623) 301-9042.
